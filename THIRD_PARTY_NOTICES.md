# Third-party components

## GLAD 2.0.8 (included)

`external/glad` contains an OpenGL 3.3 Core loader generated by
[GLAD](https://github.com/Dav1dde/glad/tree/v2.0.8). Its original source and
header notices have been preserved. See [GLAD's license](external/glad/LICENSE)
and the Khronos notice inside `external/glad/include/KHR/khrplatform.h`.

## GLFW (installed separately or downloaded by CMake)

[GLFW](https://www.glfw.org/) handles windows, keyboard input, and OpenGL
contexts. The CMake fallback downloads GLFW 3.4. GLFW is distributed under
the [zlib/libpng license](https://www.glfw.org/license.html).
